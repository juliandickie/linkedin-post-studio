# Mistake and failure hooks

## Patterns

- `"I [failed at X]."`
- `"I lost $[amount] learning this."`
- `"My biggest mistake was [specific error]."`

## Examples

1. "I lost $47K on a launch because I skipped one email."
2. "My worst business decision wasn't a bad hire. It was a great hire I didn't trust."
3. "I spent $10K on ads before I understood my offer."
4. "The $25K typo in my contract that changed how I review every doc."
5. "I turned down an investor at 24. He's worth $800M now."
6. "3 years. 180 posts. Zero clients. Here's what I did wrong."
7. "I hired a CMO who cost me $400K and 9 months. The interview mistake."
8. "I launched a product to 50K subscribers. Nobody bought. Here's the mechanism I missed."
9. "I said yes to a $120K client who cost me $80K in stress."
10. "We overdelivered ourselves out of profitability for 2 years."

## Mechanism

Negativity bias. Humans learn faster from others' failures than successes. A specific dollar amount or time cost forces the reader to picture what that loss would feel like in their own context, which creates the emotional pull that drives saves and comments.

## When to use

Use when the failure is specific and the lesson is genuinely transferable -- the post must pay off the confession with practical insight the reader can apply.

## When to avoid

Avoid humblebrag disguised as failure, where the "mistake" ends up reading as a flex -- readers recognize the pattern and trust erodes.
