# Contrarian hooks

## Patterns

- `"Unpopular opinion, [contrary take]."`
- `"[Common advice] is wrong."`
- `"Everyone says [consensus]. I disagree."`

## Examples

1. "Unpopular opinion, LinkedIn video is overrated for growth. I ran a 20-day test."
2. "Posting daily is killing your brand."
3. "Hot take, most 'thought leaders' haven't led a team of 5."
4. "Stop niching down. Niche 'up' instead."
5. "Cold outreach works. You're just bad at it."
6. "AI won't replace writers. Writers who use AI will replace writers who don't."
7. "Your morning routine isn't the problem. Your evening routine is."
8. "SDRs are dead. Long live the Account Researcher."
9. "'Follow your passion' is the worst career advice of the last 50 years."
10. "Most founders don't have a revenue problem. They have a decision-making problem."
11. "Hiring senior is usually a mistake. Here's what to do instead."
12. "Networking is overrated. Here's what actually builds a career."

## Mechanism

Pattern interrupt plus tribal signaling. Stating the opposite of received wisdom triggers a defense-of-identity response in readers who hold the conventional view — and a recognition response in readers who privately agreed but never said it out loud. Both reactions drive comments.

## When to use

Use when you can defend the take with real evidence or personal experience, not just assertion.

## When to avoid

Avoid empty contrarianism that has no supporting argument, which erodes trust over repeated posts.
