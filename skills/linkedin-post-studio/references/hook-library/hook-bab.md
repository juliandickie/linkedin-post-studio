# BAB hooks

## Patterns

- `"From [before] to [after] in [timeframe]."`
- `"Before [state]. After [state]. Bridge [mechanism]."`

## Examples

1. "From 900 followers to 85K in 14 months. One change did 80% of the work."
2. "I used to write posts for 3 hours. Now I draft them in 8 minutes."
3. "$2K/mo to $40K/mo in a year. The shift wasn't effort. It was positioning."
4. "I was cold-DM'ing 80 people a day. Now I get 80 DMs a day."
5. "Before, unread emails. After, a 38% reply rate. Bridge, a 9-word subject line."
6. "From 6 clients to 2 clients. From $12K to $45K. The math of firing your way to freedom."
7. "I was writing 5 posts a week. Now I write 2. Revenue doubled."
8. "Spent $40K on Meta ads. Generated $31K. Then I changed one thing and made $180K."

## Mechanism

Transformation promise combining social proof with actionable teaching. The Before-After-Bridge structure works because it shows a gap the reader wants to close, provides proof the gap is closeable via a specific mechanism, and implies the post will hand over that mechanism. The specific numbers anchor the before and after states, making the gap feel real rather than hypothetical.

## When to use

Use when you have concrete, verifiable numbers for both the before and after states, and when the bridge mechanism is genuinely teachable in the body of the post.

## When to avoid

Avoid when the before or after numbers are vague or unverifiable, as unsubstantiated transformation claims read as hype and reduce credibility with experienced readers.
