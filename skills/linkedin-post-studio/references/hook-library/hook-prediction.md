# Prediction hooks

## Patterns

- `"In [timeframe], [bold prediction]."`
- `"[Trend] is dead. Here's what's next."`
- `"By [year], [industry] will [transformation]."`

## Examples

1. "In 18 months, the 'LinkedIn ghostwriter' role will be dead."
2. "By 2027, every solo founder will have an AI clone."
3. "Carousels are done. Native video plus long captions is the new format."
4. "The next Justin Welsh will come from TikTok."
5. "AI will kill generic content. It will 10x specific content."
6. "In 5 years, 'personal brand' won't be a skill. It'll be a requirement."
7. "The 9-to-5 isn't dying. It's unbundling."
8. "By 2030, half of all B2B deals will close without a single sales call."
9. "Email is about to become the most expensive channel in marketing."
10. "The next decade of healthcare will be built on async + AI."

## Mechanism

Recency and relevance bias plus engages the planning brain. A specific, dated prediction signals that the author has thought further ahead than the average reader. Readers instinctively stress-test predictions against their own situation, which creates the mental engagement that drives comments and shares. The more concrete the timeframe or number, the more the brain anchors on it.

## When to use

Use when you have an informed thesis with specific reasoning and can flag hedges honestly.

## When to avoid

Avoid when the prediction is vague or untethered from evidence, as unfounded forecasts undercut the credibility that makes this format powerful.
