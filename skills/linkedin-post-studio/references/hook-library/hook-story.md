# Story hooks

## Patterns

- `"[Time period] ago, I [unusual event]."`
- `"On [specific day], I [decision moment]."`
- `"My [relation] told me [quote]. I [reaction]."`

## Examples

1. "Three years ago, I was fired over a Zoom call with 4 minutes notice."
2. "My boss said 'you're not a leader.' I quit the next Monday."
3. "I sent one cold email that turned into $180K in revenue."
4. "In 2019, I couldn't afford rent. Yesterday I wired my mom $50K."
5. "At 22, I was pitching $500 logos. At 28, I turned down a $2M offer."
6. "I wrote 847 posts before one went viral."
7. "My grandmother gave me $200 for my first business. I lost all of it in 6 months. Then I made $4M."
8. "Last Tuesday, a prospect ghosted me. Today, they signed a $320K contract."
9. "I was 9 weeks into a burnout when the email arrived."
10. "The first time I closed a 6-figure deal I was wearing pyjamas."
11. "My co-founder walked out of our Series A pitch meeting. Here's why we closed the round anyway."
12. "I got rejected from 47 investors. The 48th said yes in 11 minutes."

## Mechanism

Narrative transportation plus mirror neurons drop the scroll defense. Specific time, place, and dialog signal this is real — not a hypothetical, not a framework. Readers suspend evaluation and follow the story.

## When to use

Use when the story has a real lesson that the narrative itself delivers, not just illustrates.

## When to avoid

Avoid when vulnerability feels performative or when the "lesson" could be stated without the story at all.
