# Question hooks

## Patterns

- `"Why do [group] still [common behavior]?"`
- `"What if [counter-intuitive premise]?"`
- `"Ever wonder why [common thing is actually weird]?"`

## Examples

1. "What if your LinkedIn profile is costing you $100K a year?"
2. "Why does every 'expert' tell founders to niche down?"
3. "When was the last time you actually read a cold email to the end?"
4. "Ever wondered why the best writers never finish their drafts on day 1?"
5. "Is your content boring because you're boring, or because you're scared?"
6. "Why do smart people keep taking bad advice from loud creators?"
7. "What if your pricing is the reason clients ghost you?"
8. "Why are the best marketers I know the worst at marketing themselves?"
9. "How much would you pay to get 2 hours of your week back?"
10. "Why do most SaaS demos still start with product instead of pain?"

## Mechanism

Open-loop or Zeigarnik effect. The mind holds an unanswered question in an active state until it's resolved. That unresolved tension creates pressure to click "See more" and read through to the answer. The question also functions as a self-qualification filter: readers who feel the question applies to them are exactly the audience you want to reach.

## When to use

Use when you resolve the tension fully in the body of the post.

## When to avoid

Avoid rhetorical "Thoughts?" openers that don't lead anywhere and leave the tension open with no payoff.
