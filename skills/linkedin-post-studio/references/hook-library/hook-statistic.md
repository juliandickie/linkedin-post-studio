# Statistic hooks

## Patterns

- `"[X]% of [group] [do/fail to do] [thing]."`
- `"I analyzed [N] [posts/deals] and found..."`
- `"[Specific number] is the average [metric]. Here's what 1% do differently."`

## Examples

1. "95% of LinkedIn creators saw reach drop 50%+ in 2024."
2. "Only 1% of LinkedIn users post weekly. That's your asymmetric bet."
3. "I analyzed 215,000 LinkedIn posts. The pattern is brutal."
4. "Posts between 1,200 and 1,800 characters get 47% more comments."
5. "72% of high-performing posts end with a simple question."
6. "Hashtags reduce reach by 81% in 2025."
7. "The average cold email has a 1% reply rate. Ours gets 47%."
8. "B2B companies with a 1-week sales cycle close 3x more than those with a 4-week cycle."
9. "7 minutes is the average dwell time on our top-performing post. The platform average is 11 seconds."
10. "Only 4% of DMs on LinkedIn get a reply. Here's what the 4% do differently."
11. "94% of founders I've coached at $0 to $1M fail at the same 3 things."
12. "The top 1% of LinkedIn creators post 40% less than the bottom 50%."

## Mechanism

Authority heuristic plus specificity. Numbers signal research and pattern recognition — they imply you did the work the reader hasn't done yet.

## When to use

Use when you have a real citable data source or proprietary data that would genuinely surprise the reader.

## When to avoid

Avoid when the stat is vague or when the body doesn't pay off the specificity the hook implies.
