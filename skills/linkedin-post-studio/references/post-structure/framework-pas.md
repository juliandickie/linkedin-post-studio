# PAS (Problem, Agitate, Solve)

The fastest high-converting structure for pain-aware audiences. Widely used in SaaS, agencies, coaching, and consulting.

## Structure

**Problem.** Open with the specific symptom the reader is already feeling. One sentence, direct — a statement they'd nod at immediately.

**Agitate.** Name the wrong explanations they've already tried. Twist the knife: you've blamed the market, the product, the comp plan. This pressure makes the Solve feel like relief.

**Solve.** Deliver the reframe plus a proof point. End with a CTA offering more depth (framework, diagnostic, template).

## Full example post

> Your sales team is missing quota every quarter.
>
> You've blamed the market. The product. The comp plan. The SDRs.
>
> None of that matters if your ICP definition is wrong.
>
> Last month I helped a Series B CEO rewrite their ICP in 90 minutes. Pipeline conversion jumped from 11% to 34% in 60 days.
>
> Here's the 3-question framework we used...

## Best for

Pain-aware audiences already living the problem. Particularly effective for B2B SaaS, agency, and consulting posts where the reader has tried and failed with other solutions.

## Avoid when

The audience is not yet problem-aware — PAS requires the reader to recognize their pain in the first line. Use AIDA or SLAY instead.
