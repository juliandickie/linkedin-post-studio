# STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result)

A case-study framework used by consultants and founders. Each section sets up the next, so the Result lands with full context.

## Structure

**Situation.** Describe what the client faced before engagement — specific numbers, not vague pain. The more concrete, the more readers recognize a problem they share.

**Task.** State what needed to change — the scope, not the solution.

**Action.** Walk through specific steps taken — three or four distinct moves. Precision here separates a useful case study from a vague success story.

**Result.** Close with measurable outcomes. One strong metric beats a paragraph of adjectives.

## Full example post

> Illustrative example
>
> Situation: A SaaS company was spending $40K/month on paid ads with a 0.8% conversion rate and no attribution model.
>
> Task: Identify revenue-driving channels and cut wasted spend within 60 days.
>
> Action: Audited 3 months of data. Killed two underperforming channels. Rebuilt attribution around first-touch and last-touch. Shifted budget to the top segment.
>
> Result: Conversion rate reached 2.3% in 8 weeks. Monthly spend dropped to $24K. Revenue held flat.

## Best for

Case studies, client wins, and founder-update posts. Works best when you have real numbers the client has approved.

## Avoid when

Results are unmeasurable or the client hasn't approved the details — a vague Result erases the credibility the first three steps built.
