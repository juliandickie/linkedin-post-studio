# Hook-Story-Offer

From Ship 30 by Dickie Bush and Nicolas Cole. The atomic unit of content. Works for any post under 250 words.

## Structure

**Hook.** One line that earns the "See more" click by creating a gap the reader needs to close. Specific over vague.

**Story.** A short 3-5 paragraph account with a specific time, place, or person. Makes the Offer feel earned, not sold.

**Offer.** One CTA only — DM a keyword, comment a trigger word, or a link to a resource. Free offers close easily; paid ones need more story.

## Full example post

> Illustrative example
>
> I lost a $12,000 client in 3 minutes. Here's what I learned.
>
> Tuesday in March. Two weeks of prep. The call started well.
>
> They asked: "Can you show us results from a similar client?" I couldn't. I had results — I just hadn't documented them in a shareable format. The call ended early.
>
> That day I built a one-page case study template I've used 40 times since.
>
> Want it? Comment CASE STUDY below and I'll send it over.

## Best for

Short posts (under 250 words) for brand-awareness, community, and lead-gen. The three-part structure stops you from burying the offer or skipping the story.

## Avoid when

The idea needs more than 3-5 paragraphs to set up — the framework loses its punch when the Story sprawls.
