# PASO (Problem, Agitate, Solve, Outcome)

PAS with an explicit proof or outcome close at the end. Used by executives and founders who need credibility-first lead generation.

## Structure

**Problem.** State the specific pain the reader recognizes. One sentence, direct.

**Agitate.** Name the failed fixes or the hidden cost of inaction — the emotional escalation that earns attention for the solve.

**Solve.** Present the mechanism or reframe. The more specific, the more credible.

**Outcome.** Add a receipt: a named result, a number, or a before/after metric. This is the beat PAS omits. It closes the credibility loop for audiences who need proof before they act.

## Full example post

> Most agency owners price by the hour and wonder why clients undervalue their work.
>
> You've tried raising rates. They pushed back. You've tried retainers. They went cold.
>
> The fix is pricing by outcome, not time. Anchor your fee to the result you deliver.
>

## Best for

Executives, consultants, and founders who need the post to do credibility work before the CTA. Effective when the audience is skeptical or comparison-shopping.

## Avoid when

You lack a concrete proof point — the Outcome step without real numbers reads as self-promotion.
