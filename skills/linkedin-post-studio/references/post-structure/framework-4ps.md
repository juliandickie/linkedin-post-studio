# 4Ps (Promise, Picture, Proof, Push)

Henry Hoke's framework for longer persuasive posts where you need to hold attention across 1,500+ characters.

## Structure

**Promise.** Open with a specific, concrete outcome — name the result, the timeframe, and who achieved it. Precision earns the "See more" click.

**Picture.** Put the reader inside the result: what they're doing or no longer worrying about. Builds desire before evidence arrives.

**Proof.** Back the promise with hard numbers, screenshots, or named client results (with permission). Generic testimonials won't hold this step.

**Push.** One CTA only. Book a call, DM a keyword, click a link. Multiple asks kill conversion.

## Full example post

> Illustrative example
>
> I helped 3 consultants go from $0 to $50K/month in 90 days.
>
> Picture this: you stop chasing referrals and wake up to qualified leads in your inbox.
>
> The proof: Marcus went from $4K to $52K/month in 11 weeks. Priya closed her first $18K retainer in week 6. Both gave permission to share these numbers.
>
> DM me the word CLIENTS and I'll send you the full breakdown.

## Best for

Longer persuasive posts (1,500+ characters) for consultants, coaches, and ghostwriters building lead pipelines directly from content.

## Avoid when

Your proof is thin or client permission is unclear — a weak Proof step collapses the credibility the first three sections built.
