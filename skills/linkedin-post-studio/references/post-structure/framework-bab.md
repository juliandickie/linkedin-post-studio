# BAB (Before, After, Bridge)

Best for transformation and testimonial-style posts. The contrast between the before and after state is what earns the save.

## Structure

**Before.** Describe the situation before the change — specific numbers or behaviors, not vague descriptions. The reader needs to see themselves in this state.

**After.** State the result after the change. Concrete metrics carry the most weight. The gap between Before and After must feel significant enough to be worth reading for.

**Bridge.** Reveal the mechanism that caused the transformation. One insight, method, or decision — not a list. The Bridge is the payoff that earns DM requests and saves.

## Full example post

> Before, my client was posting 5 times a week and getting 200 views per post.
>
> After 90 days of working together, one post hit 2.4 million impressions and booked him $180K in consulting.
>
> The bridge? We stopped chasing trending formats and doubled down on his 3 weird opinions about our industry.

## Best for

Transformation posts, client testimonials, and ghostwriter/consultant case studies. Effective for brand-awareness posts where the goal is to signal results without a hard sell.

## Avoid when

The Before and After states are too similar or the Bridge is generic — a weak mechanism makes the transformation feel unearned and reduces credibility.
